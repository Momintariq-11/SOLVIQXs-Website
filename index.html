Work

Arab Foods

Fast food restaurant, serving since 2019.

  1. 1
    Understand

    The problem

    Arab Foods had no consistent visual identity and no structured marketing in place. Day-to-day operations relied on paper, with accounts and billing recorded by hand.

  2. 2
    Plan

    How we analysed it

    We reviewed the business end to end: its brand, its marketing, and the way orders, billing and accounts were handled. From that review we set out one plan covering both the brand and the systems the restaurant needed.

  3. 3
    Build

    What we built

    We built the restaurant a complete brand identity and took on its marketing from start to finish. On the operations side, we developed an order processing system and a cash inflow and outflow management system, designed around the way the restaurant works day to day.

  4. 4
    Launch & Support

    What we delivered

    Everything was delivered complete and ready to use. Both systems run on the restaurant's own web portal, Arab Foods now has a clear and consistent brand identity, and we continue to manage their accounts across different platforms.

FAQ

Questions we often get.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Shops, small and medium businesses, startups and established companies across different industries. If your business has a process that could run better or a brand that needs a stronger presence, we can help.

How much does a website or app cost?

It depends on what it needs to do. After a short conversation about your requirements, we send a clear quote that separates must-have features from optional extras — so there are no surprises.

How long does a project take?

A simple website is much quicker than a custom management system. You'll get a timeline in your project plan before any work starts, and we share progress demos along the way.

Do you build apps for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We build mobile apps for Android and iOS, whether they're for your customers or for your own staff.

Can you improve our existing website or software?

Yes. We review what you have, explain what's working and what isn't, and recommend whether to improve it or rebuild — whichever makes more sense for your business.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We help with deployment and team training, fix issues, and keep improving the system as your business grows.

Do you also handle branding and marketing?

Yes — logos and brand identity, social media management, ad campaigns and video content are all done in-house by the same team.

Do you also do AI projects?

Yes. We build AI projects too, from AI automation that handles repetitive work like sorting, replying and reporting, to AI features built into your software.
